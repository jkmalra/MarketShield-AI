# MarketShield-AI
MarketShield AI is an AI-powered margin and compliance risk intelligence system designed for small Indian e-commerce sellers.

It analyzes seller transaction data to detect pricing anomalies, margin erosion risks, and GST threshold exposure **before financial damage occurs**.

Unlike traditional seller dashboards that show raw metrics, 

MarketShield AI delivers proactive, AI-driven risk alerts and business-language insights.

🎯 Problem: Why Indian Sellers Need This

Small and mid-sized sellers on Amazon, Flipkart, and Meesho face:

- Rapid price volatility and competitor undercutting

- Thin and unstable profit margins

- Manual GST threshold tracking

- Reactive decision-making after losses occur

- Lack of affordable analytics tools

Most sellers rely on spreadsheets or static alerts.
They discover margin erosion during quarterly reviews **too late**.

MarketShield AI makes risk visible early.

### 💡 Solution

MarketShield AI transforms raw transaction data into actionable risk intelligence through:

- Pricing anomaly detection

- Margin compression analysis

- GST threshold monitoring

- Unified 0–100 risk scoring

- AI-generated risk summaries with recommendations

It provides sellers with a single, interpretable risk score and contextual insights to guide decisions.

### AI Approach

MarketShield AI uses AI where static rule-based systems fail.

1️. Time-Series Anomaly Detection

Adaptive statistical modeling identifies abnormal pricing patterns based on historical product behavior instead of fixed thresholds.

2️. Probabilistic Risk Aggregation

Multiple risk factors (pricing anomalies, margin trends, compliance exposure) are combined using weighted probabilistic modeling to generate a unified risk score.

3️. NLP-Based Risk Summaries

Risk factors are translated into business-language explanations, enabling non-technical sellers to understand and act on insights.

This ensures the system is adaptive, contextual, and decision-focused — not just metric-driven.

### Cloud Architecture

MarketShield AI follows a serverless cloud-native architecture on AWS:

- Kiro IDE – AI workflow structuring and development

- AWS Lambda – Risk analysis engine

- Amazon S3 – Transaction data storage

- Amazon DynamoDB – Risk score persistence

- Amazon SageMaker – Model hosting

- API Gateway – Client interface


The system is designed for scalable SaaS deployment while maintaining strict seller data isolation.
